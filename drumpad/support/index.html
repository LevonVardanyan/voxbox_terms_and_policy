Frequently asked questions

How do I add a sound to a pad?

Tap an empty pad. Choose a sound from the Library, pick a file from your device, or record one with the microphone. Long-press a pad that already has a sound to change its color, play mode, or edit the sound.

What are One-shot, Hold and Loop?

One-shot plays the whole sound on every tap. Hold plays only while your finger is down. Loop repeats the sound until you tap the pad again.

How do I save what I play?

Tap Record, play, then tap Record again and choose Save. Your recording appears in the Recordings tab on the home screen, where you can play, share, rename or delete it.

Which audio files can I use?

Most audio formats (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC) and the audio of MP4/MOV videos, up to 5 minutes per pad.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited pads in every project, all premium sounds in the library, and no ads. The free version allows 6 pads per project.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) or through Google Play.

Where are my projects stored?

On your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes your projects and recordings, so share the recordings you want to keep first.
