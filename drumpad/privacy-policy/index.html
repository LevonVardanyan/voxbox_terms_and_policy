Privacy Policy

My Drum Pad

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Zaza Apps ("we", "our", or "us") operates the My Drum Pad mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

a) Content you create

  • Sounds, projects and recordings — audio you import, record with the microphone, or create in the App is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Microphone — used only when you record a sound for a pad. Recordings stay on your device.

b) Automatically collected information

  • Device identifier — a random identifier generated by the App, used to associate usage analytics and purchase records with an installation.
  • Usage analytics — which features are used, session duration, app version, device model and operating system version. This does not include the audio you create.
  • Sound library activity — which library sounds are previewed or added, to show popular sounds.
  • Crash reports — diagnostic data used to find and fix bugs.
  • Advertising data — for users of the free version, our advertising partner may collect the advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads.

c) Purchase information

When you subscribe, the product ID, purchase token or transaction ID, and timestamps are sent to our server to verify the purchase with Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment card details; payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features, including the online sound library.
  • To verify and manage your subscription.
  • To show advertisements in the free version.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • To fix crashes and respond to support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) — usage analytics and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, free version only.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing — subscriptions.

4. Advertising and Consent

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can limit ad tracking in your device settings. On iOS the App asks for permission before accessing the advertising identifier. Premium subscribers see no ads.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Your sounds, projects and recordings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Analytics and purchase records are kept on our servers for as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable security measures, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your local data at any time by deleting it in the App, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App.
  • Request access to, or deletion of, analytics and purchase records linked to your installation by emailing us.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising in the App or in your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy: zazamediaapps@gmail.com
