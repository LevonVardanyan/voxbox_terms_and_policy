Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email development@zazavoxbox.com — we reply fast.

Why is my generated video silent?

Some models (Kling 2.5, Luma Ray 2, Runway Gen-4) generate silent video by design. Choose Veo 3.1, Grok Imagine or Seedance for native audio — or add AI music and voiceover to any silent clip from the result screen.

How do credits work?

AI tools are powered by credits. New users receive free starter credits, and packs from 40 to 4,000 credits are available in the app. Each tool shows its exact price before you run it — you're only charged when a generation actually runs, and failed generations are refunded automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store, not by us. On Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium until the period ends.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Google Play / the App Store, so refunds follow their policies. For Google Play, request a refund from your order history within 48 hours, or contact us and we'll help. For iOS, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

My AI generation failed — was I charged?

No. If a generation fails or is rejected by the model's content filters, your credits are refunded automatically. If you believe credits went missing, email us with your device ID (Settings → About) and we'll investigate.

What are AI characters?

Create a character once from a photo and/or a description. Then type @ in your prompt and pick them — the AI keeps the same person consistent across every video you generate.

Does Premium remove ads?

Yes — any subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly) removes all ads and unlocks every premium feature across the audio mixer, video editor and drum pad.

How do I delete my data?

VoxBox projects are stored on your device. For any server-side data tied to your device ID, email development@zazavoxbox.com and we'll erase it.
